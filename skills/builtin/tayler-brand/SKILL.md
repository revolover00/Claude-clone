# Tayler Brand Design System

## Overview
Official design guidelines and aesthetic specifications for the Tayler brand.

## Palette
- **Primary Navy**: `#1b2a4a`
- **Warm Sand**: `#f4efe6`
- **Coral Accent**: `#e06d53`
- **Sage Green**: `#6b8e76`
- **Slate Charcoal**: `#2c3539`

## Typography
- **Headings**: Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Body**: Newsreader
- **Accents**: JetBrains Mono

## Artifact Standards
All Tayler brand artifacts prioritize clear typography hierarchy, generous whitespace, and restrained accent pops.
